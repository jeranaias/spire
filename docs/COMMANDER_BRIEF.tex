  {\fontsize{22pt}{24pt}\selectfont\bfseries SPIRE}\\[2pt]
  {\fontsize{11pt}{13pt}\selectfont\itshape A Contested-Logistics Decision Engine for the Stand-in Force}
\end{center}

\vspace{-2pt}
\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\vspace{2pt}
\noindent\textbf{BLUF.} A 3d MLR S-4 forward in the First Island Chain today
cannot see Class~I, V, and~VIII in a single pane --- those classes live in
three incompatible systems while GCSS-MC focuses on Class~IX requisitioning.
SPIRE collapses GCSS-MC, DRRS-MC, and forward sensor feeds into one
role-shaped view that runs offline on a 2U box in a CONEX, with
tamper-evident audit and one-click coalition release. \textbf{TRL~4,
validated at Modern Day Marine 2026 (1st place, 30~APR 2026). Operational
pilot is the next step.}

\vspace{-2pt}
\noindent\rule{\linewidth}{0.4pt}

\section*{The Operator's Problem}
Today, a forward unit taking a Class~VIII demand spike under degraded SATCOM
has three systems open and a phone call. The planner has minutes --- not
hours --- to choose between cross-level, expedite, or emergency
requisition. The decision is unauditable; the artifact is a spreadsheet.

\section*{Capability Gap}
\begingroup
\leftskip=12pt \rightskip=12pt
\itshape\color{spireRule}
``GCSS-MC now primarily focuses on requisitioning Class~IX repair parts.''
Classes~I, V, and~VIII are managed by ``three separate incompatible
systems.'' ``Conventional sustainment remain[s] the SIF concept's greatest
vulnerability.''
\par\vspace{2pt}
\normalfont\color{spireMuted}\fontsize{9.5pt}{11pt}\selectfont

(Sept~2022). The same fragmentation is acknowledged in the
\textit{Marine Corps Concept for Logistics} (Dec~2024).
\endgroup

\section*{Approach}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Universal Ingest.} 8 formats (CSV, TSV, XLSX, JSON, JSONL,
    XML, fixed-width, EDI~X12) across 6 channels (drop, SFTP, IMAP, HTTP,
    DB~CDC, Kafka). 4 pre-built adapters: GCSS-MC ECP / UTIL / SR-Header,
    DRRS-MC C-Rating. A local Gemma~4 26B model proposes the column map for
    non-canonical sources; mappings are saved per unit.
  \item \textbf{Tamper-evident audit chain.} Every state change is SHA-256
    hash-chained and Ed25519-signed; exports as CEF for Splunk / ArcSight /
    QRadar. Inspector verification is offline, against a published key.
  \item \textbf{Local-first.} Runs on a 2U CONEX box. Vector-clock conflict
    resolution reconciles forward-node writes when SATCOM restores. No
    cloud egress.
  \item \textbf{Coalition-ready.} Live partner-scoped redaction (FVEY, JPN,
    AUS, PHL). One-click ZIP with manifest hash and release~ID.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Operational Impact}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{One pane across Class~I~/~V~/~VIII~/~IX} --- eliminates the
    cross-system reconciliation step that consumes the forward planner's
    first ten minutes of every Class~VIII~/~V surge.
  \item \textbf{Forward decisions are auditable.} The decision an S-4 made
    at H+0 is verifiable against the public key at H+30 days.
  \item \textbf{No SATCOM dependency for forward planning} --- aligns with
    the SIF requirement that sustainment be \textit{``light, flexible,
    responsive, resilient, and redundant''} (HQMC, \textit{A Concept for
    Stand-in Forces}, Dec~2021).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Maturity \& Risk}
\textbf{TRL~4.} Validated against synthetic GCSS-MC ECP / UTIL / SR-Header
data at MDM~2026; hash-chained audit verified offline. \textbf{TRL~5
requires} a sanitized real GCSS-MC pull-through from a sponsoring MEF~G-4
or MLR~S-4. ATO is not yet established; architecture is designed-in for
IL5 (audit chain, hierarchical RBAC, CAC/PIV scaffolding, no cloud egress)
but unaccredited.

\section*{Why Now}
The 39th Commandant has named logistics the \textbf{pacing function}. The
\textit{Marine Corps Concept for Logistics} (Dec~2024) calls for resilience
through redundancy, dispersion, and innovation. The data-fragmentation gap
is named in published doctrine. The window to put SPIRE in operator hands
is the FY27 POM cycle.

\section*{Path Forward --- Ask}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{90-day operational pilot} with one MEF~G-4 or MLR~S-4
    willing to point a sanitized GCSS-MC export at the ingest path.
  \item \textbf{An MCWL evaluator} to red-team SENTRY against
    operator-malpractice patterns.
  \item \textbf{A POC at MCSC PM-MC2I or DC~I\&L} to translate operator
    requirements into a contract vehicle (SBIR Phase~II or OTA).
\end{enumerate}
