## Known gaps

- **`due_in.csv` column count (82) vs. spec text (67).** The spec acceptance line is internally inconsistent: it asks for 67 columns while also requiring "same column count as the real file." The real `tmp/gcss-mc/hashed_due_in.csv` header has 82 columns, so SPIRE emits 82 to round-trip the real shape. Documented in `backend/routes/gcss_export.py`.
- **`DEFECT_CODE` long-tail beyond top-25.** The real export carries 200+ distinct defect codes; the synth generator covers the top ~50 (the union of the real top-25 plus the operator vocabulary spanning brakes/optics/comms/etc.). This is a generator-coverage gap, not a schema-alignment gap, and does **not** affect the live-file ingest path.
- **`SERVICE_REQUEST_TYPE` Jaccard ~0.33 with TVD ~0.01.** The two distributions are effectively identical at the proportion level — the Jaccard discrepancy reflects rare values (PM, Inspection) present in the synth corpus but absent from the real file's CM-only rows, not a schema mismatch.

**Reading the metrics**

- *Top-25 Jaccard*: 1.0 means the same set of top-25 values appears in both; 0.0 means disjoint.
- *Total-variation distance*: 0.0 means identical proportions across the union of top-25; 1.0 means disjoint distributions.
- A fidelity-good field has Jaccard ≥ 0.6 and TVD ≤ 0.30.
